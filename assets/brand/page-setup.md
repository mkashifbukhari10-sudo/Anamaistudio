# Kahani Nagar — Facebook Page setup

Every field below is final copy. Paste it as written; nothing here needs editing
except the email in the Contact block.

---

## Categories

Facebook allows three. Use all three, in this order.

| Slot | Category |
|---|---|
| Primary | `Video Creator` |
| Secondary | `Movie/Television Studio` — or `Animation Studio` if your locale offers it |
| Third | `Arts & Entertainment` |

**Do not choose `Media/News Company`.** News and media Pages qualify for
monetisation under the same rules but carry extra scrutiny on sourcing and
copyright. You produce everything yourself; there is no reason to opt into a
stricter review lane.

**Do not choose anything containing "Kids" or "Children".** Meta is actively
restricting minor access across its apps. Your audience is parents — say that in
the copy, not in the category.

```
KAISE BANTI HAIN

hai, magar kahani, kirdar aur sabaq poori tarah hamare apne hain.

Business & collaborations: your@email.com
```

### Why the fourth block is not optional

- Meta's originality standard is *content produced directly by the creator or
  owner of the Page*. This states plainly that you are the producer — which is
  the thing that decides both ranking and monetisation.
- It pre-empts the "AI slop" comment that will appear, and reframes the work as
  craft: you wrote the script, built the characters, directed every shot.
- It sits alongside the automatic **AI Info** label instead of contradicting it.
  The label is transparency, not a penalty; this supplies the context the label
  cannot.

## Profile picture

`kahani-nagar-avatar.svg` — open `kahani-nagar-avatar.html` in Chrome, check the
40px feed row, then click **Download 720 × 720 PNG**.

Facebook does not accept SVG. Upload the PNG.

**The rule this mark is built around:** it renders as a **40-pixel circle** in
feed. That is what decides whether it works — not the 176px on the Page. A
wordmark, a scene, or the "AS" monogram becomes a grey smudge at that size.

**Why a lantern:** it is already the signature of the films — *Roshni Ke Diye*
is named for it, and *Teen Rotiyaan* is lit end to end by a kerosene lantern and
closes on Ammi blowing it out. It says "bedtime story" without a word of text,
and it belongs to no single series, so a new cast never outdates it.

## Cover photo

`kahani-nagar-cover.html` — open in Chrome, drop in a transparent PNG of the
Sabzi Nagar cast, press `G` to check the guides, press `S` and capture.

Upload **851 × 315 PNG**. Not JPG — it will soften the wordmark.

Two crops it is built to survive:

- Mobile shows roughly the central **640** of the 851. The outer thirds are cut.
- On desktop the **profile picture covers the bottom-left**, about 96px in and
  96px up. Nothing important goes there.

Refresh the cover whenever a new series launches. It is the only free
"what's on now" slot the Page has.
